# Corporate Wallet Digital Twin V3 — technical methodology

**Version:** 3.0.0

**Point-in-time analytical snapshot:** 30 June 2026

**Implementation date:** 10 August 2026

**Team:** Corporate Wallet Digital Twin

**Team member:** Christopher Koen

## 1. Central thesis

V3 is not merely a larger wallet estimator. It is a governed decision system for a partially observed corporate financial network. A bank directly observes only the activity booked with itself. The client's total product wallet, distribution of external flows, changing treasury topology, competitor allocation and response to an RM action are latent. V3 therefore treats the task as five connected problems:

1. identify what the bank can prove from its own observations and accounting constraints;
2. reconstruct a distribution over plausible unseen networks without inventing named competitors;
3. distinguish product need from the biased event “this product is already observed and labelled”;
4. detect temporal regime changes that may indicate leakage or an event window; and
5. select RM actions and missing evidence jointly under capacity, concentration and downside risk.

The operative identity remains

\[
A_{ipt}=q_{ipt}T_{ipt},
\]

where \(A\) is activity observed by Syn Bank for client \(i\), product \(p\), time \(t\); \(q\) is bank share; and \(T\) is total wallet. Observing \(A\) does not separately identify \(q\) and \(T\). V2's identified bounds and posterior wallet remain the governed substrate. V3 adds a latent-network representation and a decision layer; it does not erase that non-identification.

## 2. Claim and evidence contract

Every displayed quantity belongs to one of five claim classes: `OBSERVED`, `IDENTIFIED_BOUND`, `POSTERIOR`, `SCENARIO` or `CAUSAL`. Syn Bank fixture activity is observed *inside the supplied simulation*. Public audited facts are E1 evidence. Anonymous provider flows generated by V3 are `SCENARIO` reconstructions with `SYNTHETIC_SIMULATION` provenance. They are never E3 observations, named banks or measured competitor share.

The V3 release fixture asserts zero measured-competitor claims and zero causal-value claims. The robust action portfolio exposes representative scenario value and lower-tail CVaR while `CAUSAL_INCREMENTAL_VALUE_WITHHELD` remains machine-readable. This prevents a sophisticated algorithm from silently upgrading the underlying evidence.

## 3. Entropy-constrained Shadow Wallet

### 3.1 Constraints

For each client-product opportunity, V3 constructs an external-flow matrix \(X\) over up to five observed Syn Bank corridors and three anonymous provider nodes. The posterior total-wallet draw \(T^{(s)}\) and observed bank activity \(A\) impose the hard mass-balance constraint

\[
\sum_{c,b} X^{(s)}_{cb}=\max(0,T^{(s)}-A).
\]

Corridor marginals come from the supplied Syn Bank simulation. Provider marginals are governed product priors. A non-negative product/corridor cost matrix expresses weak structural preference without identifying a competitor. For every one of 256 ensemble draws, V3 solves the entropy-regularised transport problem

\[
\min_{X\ge0}\;\langle C,X\rangle + \varepsilon\sum_{c,b}X_{cb}(\log X_{cb}-1)
\]

subject to the row and column marginals. Iterative Sinkhorn scaling supplies the coupling. Entropic regularisation follows Cuturi's maximum-entropy view of optimal transport and efficient matrix-scaling solution ([Cuturi, 2013](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2013/hash/af21d0c97db2e27e13572cbf59eb343d-Abstract.html)).

The design incorporates the warning from interbank reconstruction research that naive maximum entropy can produce unrealistically dense networks and understate structural uncertainty. BIS work on filling unobserved bilateral linkages motivates reporting ranges and testing alternative network densities rather than presenting one completed matrix as truth ([Anand, Craig and von Peter, 2014](https://www.bis.org/publ/work455.htm)). V3 responds with anonymous nodes, an ensemble, interval-valued edges and a `RECONSTRUCTED_NOT_MEASURED` label.

### 3.2 Output and validation

The fixture contains 100 reconstructions and 1,500 external edges. Every reconstruction validates both equations:

\[
T_{0.5}=A+U_{0.5},\qquad U_{0.5}=\sum_{c,b}X_{cb,0.5}.
\]

The maximum recorded mass-balance error is zero at exported currency precision. Normalized entropy describes how diffuse the reconstructed coupling is; it is not confidence or calibration.

## 4. Positive–unlabelled product need

Corporate product labels are selection-biased: a strong observed relationship is a plausible positive, while absence of an observed label does not prove absence of need. V3 fits a positive–unlabelled model rather than treating every unlabelled case as negative.

The transparent base learner estimates \(g(x)=P(s=1\mid x)\), where \(s\) denotes selection into the known-positive set. Under the Selected Completely At Random (SCAR) assumption,

\[
c=P(s=1\mid y=1),\qquad P(y=1\mid x)=\min\{1,g(x)/c\}.
\]

This is the Elkan–Noto correction ([Elkan and Noto, 2008](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~elkan/posonly.pdf)). V3 uses an L2-regularised logistic base learner over log observed activity, recurrence, trend, timing score, relationship breadth and country footprint. Thirty-three fixture cases are transparent known positives; all other cases remain unlabelled. The export retains the estimated selection constant and assumptions on every opportunity. Because the fixture is not a representative bank population, the result is a posterior demo score, not a production-calibrated probability.

## 5. Bayesian temporal dynamics and leakage

V3 runs online change-point detection over each 36-month client-product series. The state is the posterior distribution of run length—the months since the most recent regime change. Each new log-activity observation is evaluated under a Gaussian predictive distribution, while a constant hazard encodes the prior expected run length. This follows Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection, which performs exact online inference over the most recent change point through run-length message passing ([Adams and MacKay, 2007](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742)).

The detector emits current and recent-peak change probability, modal run length, signed level shift and monotone 30/60/90-day event probabilities. The leakage alarm is

\[
P(L)=\min\{1,P(\text{recent CP})[0.35+1.65\max(0,-\Delta)]\},
\]

and scenario flow at risk is this probability multiplied by observed decline and median reconstructed external wallet. The output label is `MODELLED_SIGNAL_NOT_CONFIRMED_LEAKAGE`. A change point without material decline is not asserted to be leakage. Production promotion requires qualified RM outcomes and calibration against named events.

## 6. Treasury graph and public sensors

The client topology joins the corporate node to observed fixture corridors and reports a Treasury Complexity Index derived from relationship breadth, corridor diversity and shadow-network entropy. It is explicitly labelled a Syn Bank simulation topology. GLEIF is registered as an identity and parent-relationship sensor but is not silently entity-resolved. The official GLEIF API supports legal-entity and parent/child relationship searches ([GLEIF API](https://www.gleif.org/en/lei-data/gleif-api/)); production identity resolution requires deterministic matching and human review.

The registry also defines point-in-time adapters for:

- SARS monthly trade statistics by country and commodity ([SARS trade reports](https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/trade-statistics/reports/));
- UN Comtrade reporter/partner/product/flow data ([UN Comtrade API](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/un-comtrade-api/));
- JSE SENS corporate announcements, subject to JSE licensing ([JSE market announcements](https://www.jse.co.za/market-data/market-announcements)); and
- SARB versioned macroeconomic and financial time series ([SARB online query](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/statistics/releases/online-statistical-query)).

The offline build registers these sensors but does not claim they are connected. Each adapter must retain query parameters, release/vintage, availability timestamp, revision lineage, licence and entitlement domain.

## 7. Robust RM portfolio

Ranking opportunities independently ignores scarce banker time and portfolio concentration. V3 constructs 512 common commercial scenarios per candidate. Each scenario varies wallet scale, product conversion and leakage urgency around the governed V2 contestable-contribution calculation. For candidate \(j\), the robust score combines mean scenario value and lower 10% conditional value at risk:

\[
S_j=(1-\lambda)E[V_j]+\lambda\operatorname{CVaR}_{0.10}^{\text{lower}}(V_j),\quad \lambda=0.55.
\]

A deterministic constrained selector chooses at most 12 actions, one per client, four per product and four per sector. The exported portfolio uses 12 slots and satisfies every constraint. Glencore and BHP Trade Finance remain selected, but the optimizer does not hard-code a product winner. Because pricing and response curves are representative, the result is a robust *scenario portfolio*, not an optimal bank policy or causal value estimate.

## 8. Decision-directed evidence and RAG

Ordinary RAG retrieves documents because they are semantically relevant. V3 retrieves evidence only if it is expected to change a capacity-constrained decision enough to justify cost and delay. For candidate evidence \(e\),

\[
\operatorname{NetVOI}(e)=E[U(a^*(D,e))-U(a^*(D))]-C_e-L_e.
\]

The committed planner evaluates E3 multibank observation, approved rate-card and E2 attestation requests for selected actions. Expected interval narrowing and rank-flip probability approximate decision benefit. The queue contains only positive-net-VOI items up to capacity eight. This is aligned with cost-sensitive value-of-information feature acquisition, where acquisition is justified by reduced downstream decision loss rather than relevance alone ([Bilgic and Getoor, 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.3881)).

No external retrieval is autonomous. Every selected request names its required approval. Public retrieval remains entitlement-aware, licence-aware, point-in-time and human reviewed.

## 9. Evidence-cited GenAI briefing

V3 compiles a sealed claim pack containing Syn Bank observed activity, approved public citations, model claims with support identifiers, selected/deferred action state and missing E3, finance and causal evidence. The existing three-provider gateway may transform only that pack into schema-constrained prose. The prompt contract forbids external tools, publication, CRM action and the phrases “measured competitor share”, “causal uplift” and “optimal target share”. Every number must survive deterministic validation and every public claim must resolve to a fact ID and page citation. When a provider is absent or validation fails, the deterministic brief is returned.

## 10. Software architecture and interfaces

V3 is additive under `src/wallet_twin_v3`; it consumes stable V2 point-in-time evidence, bounds, posterior and economics contracts. The FastAPI process retains every `/v1` route and adds:

- `GET /v3/opportunities`
- `GET /v3/clients/{clientId}/latent-network`
- `GET /v3/leakage`
- `GET /v3/action-portfolio`
- `GET /v3/evidence-acquisition`
- `GET /v3/opportunities/{opportunityId}/brief`
- `GET /v3/models/validation`

OpenAPI and 22 JSON Schemas are generated from code. Object-level authorization uses the deny-by-default V2 principal and access-decision log. The workbench adds the V3 Decision Lab but preserves the V2 Evidence Twin and release gates.

## 11. What the V3 demonstration establishes—and what it cannot

The implementation establishes reproducible mechanics, exact accounting reconciliation, deterministic capacity controls, point-in-time contracts, claim separation and a decision-relevant demo over all 20 clients. It does not establish external validity for a real bank population. Production remains blocked until the bank supplies representative E3 multibank observations, approved product economics, production identity/entitlement infrastructure, licensed public feeds, live-provider adjudication, qualified RM outcomes, a supervised pilot, a randomized trial and clean shadow operation. Those gates are data and operating facts; code cannot fabricate them.
