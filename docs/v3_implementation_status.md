## Release statement

The V3 client demonstration is complete as a governed representative decision laboratory. It may be used to demonstrate the operating model, algorithms, controls and user experience. It may not be represented as a live bank production system, measured competitor intelligence, approved pricing, a calibrated leakage detector or demonstrated causal uplift.
